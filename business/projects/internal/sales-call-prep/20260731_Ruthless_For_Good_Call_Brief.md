- **Size:** 2-person investing team (Aaron Walker, Michael Ladipo) plus Martina as fractional COO — solo / 2-5 tier.
- **Stage:** Established fund manager (5 years, 16-17 portfolio investments) but very lean operating team.

- **Notable:** Martina Madrid Sebring is fractional COO here AND already Blue Tusk's primary contact at Maycomb Capital, an active client — she has firsthand experience with the diagnostic process before this call starts.

## Anticipated Pains
1. With only Aaron and Michael running the fund, portfolio monitoring and LP reporting across 16+ investments likely relies on manual spreadsheet tracking rather than a structured system.
2. As a mission-first, impact-focused fund, RFG almost certainly has to pull outcome and diversity data from its portfolio companies for reporting — the same manual data-transfer bottleneck Blue Tusk is already solving at Maycomb (P-007).
3. Deal sourcing and diligence for pre-seed/seed with a 2-person team is likely tracked ad hoc in email and spreadsheets rather than a CRM, risking deals going cold between touchpoints.
4. Fund administration and compliance (K-1s, capital calls, SEC filings for the registered fund entity) is probably handled reactively rather than on a documented cadence.
5. Hands-on founder support — RFG's stated differentiator — likely consumes real Aaron/Michael time in ways that are valuable but hard to scale or systematize.

---

## Sharp Questions to Ask
- "With just the two of you running the fund, how are you tracking touchpoints and diligence notes across your pipeline right now?"
- "How do you currently pull together portfolio data for LPs, or for anything like an impact report?"
- "What does fund administration look like today — are K-1s and capital calls handled in-house or outsourced?"
- "What's taking the most time in your operation right now that you wish wasn't?"
- "What have you already tried to fix that?"
- "Martina's been in the middle of similar back-office work with another client recently — has any of that started crossing over into how RFG runs day to day?"

---

## What to Listen For
Listen for manual, recurring reporting or portfolio-tracking work Aaron or Michael describe with visible frustration, and for signs Martina is already pushing automation at RFG the way she is at Maycomb. If she's the one raising the idea unprompted, that's the strongest signal she's primed to champion this internally.

---

## Red Flags
A two-person shop this lean may have very limited paid-engagement budget — likely enters as a free or in-kind diagnostic, same as Maycomb. Martina's bandwidth is split across at least two active client relationships, which could slow follow-through even if the fit is strong.
