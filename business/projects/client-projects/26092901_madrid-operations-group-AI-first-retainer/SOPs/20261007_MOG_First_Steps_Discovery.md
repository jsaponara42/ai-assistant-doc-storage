### 5. Your non-negotiables

- **Client communication.** Nothing goes to a client, prospect, or partner without my review. Drafts are fine; sends are mine.
- **Money.** Pricing, rates, scope changes, invoices going out, and any payment all need my approval. Ashley can prep invoices; she does not send them without sign-off
- **Calendar.** Ashley can propose and queue work blocks, but conflicts come to me. Ashley flags conflicts rather than resolving them.
- **Anything public.** LinkedIn posts, website changes, and marketing materials are never published without me.
- **Facts from transcripts.** Fireflies summaries are not reliable for names, dollar figures, who said what, or confirmed decisions. Anything accuracy-sensitive gets checked against the full transcript, and I review before it goes into a client record.
- **Sensitive topics.** Personnel issues, legal or compliance questions, and anything touching a client's finances or their customers' data stay with me and, where needed, the client's own attorney or advisor.
- **Client confidentiality.** Each client's information stays in its own space. No client's data should show up in another client's workspace, draft, or report. RFG already has a separate Notion workspace from MOG, and I want that separation built into the design. **[r][s]**
