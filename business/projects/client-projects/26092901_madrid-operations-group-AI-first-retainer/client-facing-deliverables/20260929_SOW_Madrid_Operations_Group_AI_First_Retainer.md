# Statement of Work

**Blue Tusk LLC & Madrid Operations Group**

Date: September 29, 2026
Client Contact: Martina Madrid Sebring, Founder & Principal (martina@madridops.com)
Blue Tusk Contact: John-Carlos Saponara (jcsaponara@bluetuskllc.com)

## Project Overview

Blue Tusk will provide Madrid Operations Group with a monthly AI and automation retainer. The retainer covers two things: building an AI-first operation for Madrid Operations Group in parallel with its current business, and on-demand "phone a friend" support for AI and automation questions that come up in Madrid Operations Group's client work.

## Scope of Work

**Engagement:** AI-First Retainer (monthly)

### 1. Building Madrid Operations Group's AI-First Operation

Blue Tusk will design and build backend and project management automation for Madrid Operations Group, with the goal of maximizing focus time for client work and winning new business. This includes:

- Discovery into how Madrid Operations Group operates today, so each part of the business can be rebuilt with intent
- Recommending and setting up a new, AI-native set of tools, built in parallel with current systems rather than integrated into them
- Building automations that reduce manual administrative and project management work (for example, turning call transcripts into notes, next steps, and a weekly task list)
- Reviewing Madrid Operations Group's current subscriptions, so each tool in use serves a clear purpose

### 2. Phone a Friend

Email-based support for AI and automation questions arising from Madrid Operations Group's client work, including:

- Quick reviews of ideas, workflows, and processes
- Feasibility assessments: whether something is worth automating, and roughly how
- Basic coaching through AI builds Madrid Operations Group is doing itself
- Direct help with prompting, including drafting prompts when needed

**Format:** Build work is carried out on an ongoing basis, with working sessions scheduled as needed. Phone a friend requests are handled by email. Blue Tusk may speak directly with Madrid Operations Group's clients when useful, coordinated through Madrid Operations Group.

## What's Not Included

- Branded or "pretty" materials to be shown to Madrid Operations Group's clients. These may be provided for a small one-time fee, quoted in advance.
- Build, implementation, or deployment work performed directly for Madrid Operations Group's clients. Such work is available as a separate engagement.
- Automation of LendForGood's current invoice process, given its expected backend rebuild. Coaching on a stopgap Madrid Operations Group builds itself with Claude (locally, via Claude Code or Cowork) is available under Phone a Friend.
- Direct costs for tools, software subscriptions, and AI usage (see Direct Costs below).

## Timeline and Term

The retainer runs for a **3-month minimum term**, from **September 29, 2026** through **December 29, 2026**. At the end of the minimum term, Blue Tusk and Madrid Operations Group will decide together whether to continue, reduce, or end the service.

**Pacing:** Because this engagement is offered at a discounted rate, Blue Tusk will prioritize higher-paying client work first, and the pace of build work may vary from month to month. Phone a friend availability by email is expected to remain consistent.

## Direct Costs

Madrid Operations Group is responsible for direct costs of the tools built under this engagement, including AI tokens and software subscriptions. These are estimated at **approximately $200/month**. This is an estimate only; actual costs may be higher or lower. Blue Tusk will consult Madrid Operations Group before any recurring spend is activated.

## Referral Terms

Standard Blue Tusk referral terms apply to clients Madrid Operations Group refers to Blue Tusk:

- **10% commission** on referred project work over $3,000
- **5% commission** on referred retainers over $2,500/month, for 24 months

## Governing Terms

This Statement of Work operates under the Master Services Agreement (MSA) between Blue Tusk LLC and Madrid Operations Group, which has already been signed. The MSA governs all terms and conditions applicable to this engagement, including confidentiality, liability, and ownership.

## Investment

**Monthly Retainer: $1,500/month** (3-month minimum)

**Billing:** The retainer is billed automatically each month, beginning September 29, 2026.

Blue Tusk's retainers typically start at $2,500/month. This engagement carries a discounted rate because building an AI-first operation in parallel with an existing business has research and development value to Blue Tusk.

Direct costs (estimated ~$200/month) are paid by Madrid Operations Group and are separate from the retainer.

## Acceptance

Madrid Operations Group's written acceptance of this Statement of Work, by signature or email reply, confirms the terms above, effective September 29, 2026.
