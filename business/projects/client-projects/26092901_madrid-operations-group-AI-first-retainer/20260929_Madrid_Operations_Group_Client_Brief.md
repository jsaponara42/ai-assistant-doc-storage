## 7. Problem Register

### P-001 — Call follow-up is manual (People / Processes)
- **Area:** Processes / Tools.
- **Problem:** Since starting at Maycomb, Martina is on many calls. She can turn transcripts into notes and pull out next steps into Notion, but every step is still manual. She has no single, reliable view of "here are your to-dos this week."
- **Current Thinking:** AI helps with each step, so running the steps by hand is acceptable.
- **Reframe:** Hand-carrying output between AI steps is still manual work. The whole chain — transcript to notes to tasks to weekly plan — should run as one system, like a chief of staff.
- **Approach:** Build an AI chief-of-staff workflow on the new MOG stack. Status: Not started.

### P-002 — Tedious client execution crowds out strategic work (People / Processes)
- **Area:** Processes / People.
- **Problem:** Martina is "drowning" in automatable client work: LFG loan admin (covering for an absent employee), HubSpot task clean-up, Maycomb AP validation, San Antonio tax data pulls. This leaves little time for the strategic work she's hired for.
- **Current Thinking:** Clients can't afford outside help, so Martina absorbs the execution herself.
- **Reframe:** Expert guidance on *what* to automate and *how*, with exact prompts, lets Martina build small automations herself quickly — clients get the benefit without a separate budget line.
- **Approach:** Phone-a-friend support, starting with JC's top 2–3 picks in the LFG loan admin process. Status: Not started — waiting on the loan admin list from Martina.
