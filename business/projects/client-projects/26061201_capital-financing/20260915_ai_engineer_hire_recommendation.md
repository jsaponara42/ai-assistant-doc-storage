# AI Engineer Hire + JC Management Retainer — Recommendation

## Summary
Recommendation to present to Howie: hire the outside AI engineer at the already-quoted ~$3,000/month rate for execution, and retain JC separately at $1,500/month as the engineer's manager and the strategic decision-maker on what gets built. Resolves the discovery brief's "re-evaluate need for AI engineer hire" checkpoint and the open AI-engineer-hire question sitting in [[xx_howies_wants]].

## Context
Evaluating whether to hire a full-time AI engineer was part of this engagement's original scope (see [[20260612_discovery-brief-capital-financing]] — "End of Month 4 — re-evaluate need for AI engineer hire based on progress"). Howie has separately been fielding an outside pitch for this: a contact named Alex Miranda offering an AI engineer placement at ~$3,000/month, with a more senior/expensive engineer named Frank as the actual candidate (logged in [[xx_howies_wants]], Open Questions — "Recurring 'drop an AI agent into Salesforce' confusion"). Howie's own CMO overview document ([[20260911_marketing_CMO_hire_reference]]) already lists JC internally as "CTO / AI Engineer," so this formalizes a frame he's already using rather than introducing a new one.

## Content

**The core argument:**
- The automations identified through this engagement can be built by a dedicated engineer for meaningfully less than JC's own implementation rate — same output, much lower cost. Not a quality gap, a pricing-efficiency gap.
- The gap in hiring an outside engineer alone: they start with zero business context. They won't know the org, the workflows, or the institutional knowledge this engagement has spent months building — and won't get there as fast or as well as JC did, because deep client/business context isn't their specialty. Building, not context-gathering, is what they're good at (and plausibly better than JC at the actual build).
- Proposed structure: hire the engineer at ~$3,000/month for execution; retain JC at $1,500/month as the person who sets strategy and is the one the engineer actually reports to.

**Cost comparison:**
| Option | Monthly cost |
|---|---|
| JC implements automations directly at JC's own rate | ~$10,000/mo |
| Full-time hire with JC's combined skill set (strategy + build) | ~$15,000–20,000/mo |
| Engineer ($3,000) + JC managing/directing ($1,500) | ~$4,500/mo |

**Value to Howie beyond price:**
- Keeps him out of engineer-management entirely — something he's explicitly said isn't CEO work he wants to be doing.
- Preserves the business-context risk (blank-slate hire problem) by routing it through JC's oversight instead of Howie having to transfer that knowledge himself.
- Gets a non-US engineering rate combined with the strategic/institutional-knowledge layer that rate alone doesn't buy.
