# Howie Call Prep — Time Value & Wishlist Process Detail

## Summary

Call prep for a 1:1 with Dr. Howie Golden (CEO). Two goals: (1) understand what's actually the highest-value use of his time, so automation and delegation work targets what matters most rather than whatever's loudest, and (2) get concrete process detail behind the items on his 2026-08-07 wishlist, since several of them are still too vague to scope. No Easy SOP re-training segment on this call — Howie already dictates freely and at length (the "Ghost Notes" habit); this call is about direction and specificity, not teaching him a method.

## Context

Howie's IOV is explicit in the discovery brief: restore CEO bandwidth for visionary work and top-of-funnel selling, step out of day-to-day sales management, build a self-managing sales structure. He's currently doing four jobs at once — visionary, #1 salesperson, default sales manager, and conference logistics operator — and that overload is the root of PO-005. His wishlist (logged in [[xx_howies_wants]] items #11–#13, plus the Claude and email items) reads as a mix of genuinely scoped automation asks and vaguer aspirations that need a real conversation to become buildable. Source grounding: [[20260612_discovery-brief-capital-financing]] (IOV, Howie profile, PO-005, PO-003), [[20260616-Workflow-Map-Capital-Financing-merged]] (Salesforce Daily Routine, Workflow 5 — Conference Marketing), and [[xx_howies_wants]].

## How to run this call

- **Howie is a Persuader profile** — high-functioning, results-only, allergic to micromanagement, self-aware about his own weaknesses, subjective/intuitive decision-maker. He does not need convincing of anything; he needs the structural translation handed back clean. Keep questions sharp and move fast — he types ninety words per minute and doesn't need a lot of framing before each question.
- **He acts before the framework is ready if there's no structure in front of him.** If the conversation starts drifting toward him deciding something on the spot (a hire, a platform, a Julius decision), it's fine to let him think out loud, but don't let JC's own questions accidentally lock in a decision that hasn't gone through discovery yet — several of these are explicitly still "maybe."
- **Don't raise the CEO-intervenes-in-underwriting-files pattern on this call.** It's a real breakpoint (see workflow map, Cross-Workflow Observations #1) but it's a Christy/operations-side issue, not a time-value or wishlist topic — raising it here would blur two separate conversations. Leave it for whenever operational governance is actually being discussed with him directly.
- **He wants a roughly weekly recap and expects acknowledgment on unprompted emails**, not necessarily a reply. Not this call's topic, but worth a quick temperature-check if there's a natural opening.

## Part 1 — Time & Value (~20 min)

**Primary:**
- If you could hand off everything except the two or three things only you can do, what would those two or three things be?
- Walk me through last week — where did your time actually go, hour by hour if you can remember it, versus where you wish it had gone?
- What's the one thing you're doing right now that you know, deep down, someone else could do just as well or better, if you had the right system behind it?

**Follow-ups:**
- Of the four jobs you're currently doing — visionary, top salesperson, sales manager, conference logistics — which one is the biggest actual drain on you, versus which one you'd keep even if you didn't have to?
- When you imagine having your time back, what does a "good week" actually look like — more selling, more strategy, more travel, more family time, something else entirely?
- Is there a task you keep doing yourself not because you have to, but because it's genuinely faster than explaining it to someone else? That's often the first thing worth automating.

## Part 2 — Wishlist process specifics

Go through each item below only as deep as time allows — these are in rough priority order based on what's already scoped versus what's still vague. Don't try to resolve any of these on the call; the goal is enough detail to scope, not a decision.

### Julius — automate vs. reposition (wishlist item 3)

- *Primary:* Kaz has told you multiple times Julius's outreach work is automatable. What's actually stopped that from happening so far?
- *Primary:* You mentioned Josh talked about having Julius become your personal assistant, like "Jan" was. Who's Jan, and what did that role actually look like?
- *Follow-up:* If Julius's current outreach work got fully automated, what would you actually want him doing instead — is "personal assistant" a specific job description in your head, or more of a general idea right now?
- *Follow-up:* Kaz's own recommendation has been to cut Julius rather than reposition him — worth knowing directly: is keeping Julius on in some role important to you personally, or purely a question of what's most useful for the business?

### KPI dashboard Kaz already built (wishlist item 4)

- *Primary:* Walk me through what you actually do with the KPI dashboard when you look at it — what are you checking for, and what decision does it help you make?
- *Follow-up:* When you first reviewed it with Kaz, what did you ask him to fix? Is that fix still holding up, as far as you know?
- *Follow-up:* If it got rolled out to the team tomorrow, what would you want them to actually do differently because of it?

### Salesforce platform decision — Professional vs. Enterprise, and Segue (wishlist item 5)

- *Primary:* Where's your head at right now on Segue versus improving the Salesforce build you already have? Has anything changed since your last exchange with them went nowhere?
- *Follow-up:* If Segue re-engages and gives you a real functionality overview, what would actually change your mind one way or the other?
- *Follow-up:* Is there a timeline pressure here — something that makes this decision urgent — or is it fine to let it sit until the KPI/opportunity mapping work is further along?

### Sales team accountability without a dedicated manager (wishlist item 6)

- *Primary:* You've said you're not a people manager and don't have the headcount for one — what does "accountable" actually look like to you in practice? A report you check? A conversation that happens automatically? Something else?
- *Follow-up:* Of the reports that already exist (Prospects, Top Prospects, Top Companies, the two inactive reports), which ones do you actually find yourself using, and which ones do you mostly ignore?

### Email automation (wishlist item 2)

- *Primary:* When you say "automation for emails," what's the actual email problem you're picturing — your own inbox, the outbound cadence Julius runs, something else entirely?
- *Follow-up:* Is there a specific email task you find yourself repeating that you'd want gone first?

### Claude "advanced" / AI working in the background (wishlist item 1)

- *Primary:* Tell me more about what you've heard your friends' Claude setups doing — what specifically is it analyzing or tapped into, as you understand it?
- *Follow-up (framed as informational, not a correction of him personally):* I want to make sure I get you the real version of this rather than the rumor — happy to walk through what Claude can and can't actually do in the background once we're past this list, so you know exactly what's possible versus not.

*(Note to JC: confirm current product capabilities before answering this one directly on the call — don't answer from memory.)*

### Conference logistics (tied to Part 1, item 5 in the wishlist context)

- *Primary:* Walking through a typical conference from your side — selecting the event, attending, capturing contacts — which of those steps genuinely needs to be you, and which is just "has always been you"?
- *Follow-up:* If someone else ran logistics for the next conference, what's the one thing you'd still want to personally control?
