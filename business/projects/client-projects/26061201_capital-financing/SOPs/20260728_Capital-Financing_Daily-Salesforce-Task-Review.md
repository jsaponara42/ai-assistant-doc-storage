## 1. Purpose

This SOP documents the daily routine for reviewing and managing Salesforce tasks at Capital Financing. It covers how the CEO starts each day by working through the task list, following up with law firm contacts, tracking onboarding progress, and maintaining consistent outreach to prospects and inactive accounts. The goal is to ensure that no opportunity falls through the cracks and that follow-up cadence remains consistent and reliable.

This document is written for someone stepping into the CEO role with a general understanding of sales operations, but no prior knowledge of Capital Financing's specific Salesforce setup or workflows.

## 2. Background & Key Concepts

**What is the Salesforce Task Review?**
The Salesforce Task Review is the daily morning routine where the CEO reviews all current and overdue tasks in Salesforce, contacts the relevant people, and either completes or re-schedules each task. It is the single most important daily activity at Capital Financing.

**Why are Tasks Critical?**
Tasks in Salesforce contain all due dates and overdue dates for every active relationship. They serve as the primary organizational tool — without them, follow-up cadences break down and opportunities are lost. Most law firms do not send their first referral within the requested one-week window after onboarding; sometimes not for months. The task system exists specifically to catch these cases proactively.

**What is the Onboarding Follow-Up?**
After a strategy call, the CEO conducts an onboarding session with the law firm. Christy sends notes after the call indicating completion. The CEO then checks whether the firm has sent their first referral — which Capital Financing requests within the first week. This follow-up task is critical because most law firms do not send cases promptly after onboarding.

**What is the Inactive Report?**
A Salesforce report that lists all accounts that have gone inactive — either for case financing or pre-settlement financing. These accounts are reviewed monthly (and also by Julius behind the scenes) to determine whether outreach should continue, pause, or resume.

**What is the Top Companies / Top Prospects Report?**
A Salesforce report showing the firm's highest-value law firms (top companies) and active leads (top prospects). These are reviewed daily alongside the task list to keep the CEO abreast of priority relationships.

## 5. Schedule

The task review is a **daily morning routine** — performed every business day when the CEO walks in and starts up Salesforce. A secondary **monthly review** covers the inactive report.

- **Daily (morning):** Review current and overdue tasks, contact relevant people, check off or re-schedule each task.
- **Monthly (as needed):** Review the inactive report for both case financing and pre-settlement financing, and follow up with those accounts.

## 7. Exception Handling

**Law Firm No Longer Useful**
If, after multiple follow-up attempts, a law firm is determined to be no longer useful or worth the time, remove the task from Salesforce entirely. Do not let it sit as a zombie task.

**Contact Unresponsive**
If a contact consistently does not respond to emails or calls, document this in Salesforce notes and consider whether to remove the task or reduce follow-up frequency.

**Christy's Onboarding Notes Missing or Delayed**
If Christy has not sent post-call notes after a strategy call, check with her directly to determine whether onboarding is actually complete. Do not assume completion without written confirmation.

**Julius's Inactive Account Follow-Up Gaps**
Julius falls months behind in his cadence, which defeats the consistency needed for inactive account outreach. This should be addressed through automation (templated, creative emails sent on a consistent schedule) rather than relying on manual outreach.

**Task Overruns**
Not all tasks get completed in time and they tend to run overdue. This is expected. Prioritize current and overdue tasks daily and create new follow-up tasks with realistic due dates based on urgency.
