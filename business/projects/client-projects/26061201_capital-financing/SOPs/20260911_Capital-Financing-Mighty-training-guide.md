Mighty Training Instructions:

- Sales and Account Management team will need to review their weekly activities such as approved and denied advances.
- Although approved funding are what you are commissioned on, denied are equally as important so you can see what accounts you manage that may be referring to you.
- Sales reps need to understand that denials are due to a good reason.  It is not to be assumed when case are denied that we are to conservative or difficult. We are in the business of funding all cases that fit within our criteria of regulations and type of case. If we deny, know there is a good reason and that should lie within the plaintiff case profile for you to review. 
- End of month reporting is critical and will not only be used to provide verification of commissions, but will be used during your end of month meeting with executives.
- Use Saved Reports to easily export reports
- Use Analytics to look at big picture of your accounts but also be able to go more detailed. It is recommended that all detailed reporting be done through the Reports Tab not Analytics.
- Mighty is not to be used similarly as Salesforce. Salesforce will be able to provide detailed information about individual firms for meetings, but Mighty is our financial software that is going to be best for details.  



Upon Logging in to Mighty:

- You will see Applications, Portfolio, Reports, Analytics, Contacts, and Tranche Summaries across the top.

- For purposes of your role, you will only use Applications, Reports, and Analytics.

  

Applications Tab:

- Applications:

- Case Owner: This should stay on All
- Application Status: This tells you what the pipeline looks like that is being worked on the Ops team.  

- New: Means just came in and assigned with minimal information
- Processing: Means Intake Team is working on this by communication with client or law firm
- Underwriting: Intake has completed their work and have passed this onto Underwriting Dept
- Approved: These are now in contracting where we go over contracts, discuss delivery method, and then all contracts are sent electronically to plaintiff and law firm.  Please do not look at the Request Amount as the approved amount. The approved amount can be found if you open the case.

  

Reports Tab:

- Viewed By: You can choose Lien or Case. I prefer Lien as a search criteria but the difference is a Lien is the individual advance we make in a Case. A Case is the actual Date of Loss and any lien we approve will be included in the Case. 
- All: These are any lien we have reviewed, approved, denied, or cancelled
- Non-Funded Application: Never used it
- Open Application: You don’t need to review here as you can select Applications Tab
- Rejected: These are all those we have denied and you can filter the dates you would like to review by selecting the filter feature below that says Application Created at Date Range.  I.E. Don’t get confused with Funded Date Range as Rejected never was Funded and it would be Rejected in the Application Stage so you search during that Date Range.
- All Liens: Same as All
- Open Liens: These are any that we funded but not settled. You set the Funded Date Range and you can see what we funded that are open. Not a feature you would need to use.
- Closed: These are all Cases/Liens closed. You don’t need this filter
- All Account Managers should fusing on All Liens and Rejected Status Filters.
- All Account Managers should focus on Funded Date Range (Approved) and Application Date Range (Denied/Cancelled) 

  

Filtering Under Report Tab:

When running reports, you can be as detailed as you want.   All the filter areas on the left side are your friend.   You would use Funded or Application Created Date Ranges for your searches.  You would filter the state of your territory and lead source category. This will show the state you are in the cases we approved, denied, or cancelled and how the case came to us whether it is Law Firm Referral, Internet, Client Referral, Returning Client, or Medical Office.  Remember you are only commissioned on Referred by Law Firm not repeat client that initiated a new loan on a new case. 

  

When running weekly or end of the month reports you should filter with these instructions:

- Select All Liens
- Select Funded Date Range: This will allow you to see the approved advances during the date range you have selected.
- Select State of Incident: Should indicate all the attorneys in your state you work with.  *It is a good idea at the end of the month to run a report for All Funding for the company just in case an attorney you work with referred a case but the state of the incident was a different state. You are commissioned on the Law Firm referral not the state for which the accident occurred. 
- Lead Source Category: If there are multiple sources that month which a client came to Capital Financing, you can see all the ways and select Attorney Referral to see which ones may be commissionable.   The other sources are valuable as a tool to allow you to follow up with that law firm and try to make them a referring law firm.  
- Not other fields are necessary

  

Another way to run weekly or end of the month reports is by using the Saved Reports (in Green) at the top right. Follow these instructions:

- Click Saved Reports
- Client End of Month (all configured with the necessary criteria)
- Select at the Left Funded Date Range you would like to view (this is important and it can be any range, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or for months in the rear).
- Don’t be confused with the End of Month. This is simply the title to run end of the month based on the criteria we saved that we want but that information can be beneficial whenever you want and if you select the “Configure” tab, you will see all the saved Criteria for the End of the Month. Your key is the Funded Date Range as all the other filters are pre-saved with the saved search.

  

Export:

- Once you have your report filtered you can now export
- This will export to Excel
- You may Delete the unnecessary columns that will be defaulted such as: Lien Id, Case Id, Case Id
- Keep all the other filtered fields
- This report will be used at the end of the month to report to Executives in your monthly meeting.
- You will copy and paste this report into the Document provided to you for your monthly meeting and reporting. Which will include your Goals v. Production Stats Comparison Yr over Yr.  You will drop this report in the Mighty Details staff where each month you will apply them.  

  

Analytics:

- Just another way for you to review your Approved advances. This will not show you any denied or cancelled as this will only be in the Reports Tab.
- Click Analytics
- Click Custom Reports
- You will see 3 reports
- Top Left: Filter

- Select your date range
- Select your state
- If this doesn’t work, put ticket into Support@mighty.com that the Analytics Tab is not working. This happens sometimes and needs them to refresh.
- Once Filtered: Each report will adjust

- I prefer to work off the bottom left report

- You can then right from the report click on any of the blue area to realign by order you want.
- I like to go from highest to lowest for the Cases so I see who are top performers
- You will see the total cases and then total liens. Total liens should be larger as there is typically more than 1 lien in each case.
- You can see the value of the law firm by the dollar we have funded.  Clearly you can do less cases and have more money approved based on size of advances.
- It is important to know that these are ALL cases we have funded but doesn’t show you the referral source. Do not assume these cases are only those referred to us by the firm as these are simply cases we funded during the date range you filteed.  

  

- The top report is nice detail if you like to review the # of the liens for a particular plaintiff. During your filtered date range for a 90 day or 6 month period, you can see the amount of advances a particular plaintiff receives.  

  

Can you export from Analytics?

- All reports you see can be exported
- If you hover over the top right corner of each report you will see the hamburger sign of 3 lines going horizontal.  You can do a pivot table there or just download and do a pivot table in Excel.
