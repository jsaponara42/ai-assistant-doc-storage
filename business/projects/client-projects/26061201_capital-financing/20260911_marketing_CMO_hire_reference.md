**1. Company Overview**

Capital Financing is a national pre-settlement and case expense funding firm serving personal injury law firms. The company is owned and led by Howie Golden, who oversees both the operational and sales sides of the business.

**1.1 Product Lines**

- **Pre-Settlement Funding:** advances against a plaintiff's pending personal injury case, sourced through referring law firms.  Provides plaintiffs a loan.  These services come to CF one of two ways:  1) referral from law firm is #1 value and most common 2) Internet searches but less common as we are not directly marketing other than SEO.  
- **Case Expense Financing (CEF):** financing for the costs of litigating a case. CEF is a newer services that I would love to grow.  It is a direct benefit to the law firm.  Requires a few steps to get law firms ready to refer.  

**1.2 Business Model: Law Firms First**

Capital Financing's model is built around law firms, not plaintiffs. The company's own funding customers (plaintiffs) are almost always referred in directly by a law firm that already has a relationship with Capital Financing. This is a deliberate positioning choice: Capital Financing does not want to compete with the private equity-backed companies that market pre-settlement funding directly to consumers/plaintiffs. Instead, the goal is for law firms to refer their clients directly and for marketing dollars to go toward building awareness and trust with law firms — at conferences and through other law-firm-facing channels — rather than toward consumer acquisition.   This is where Financial Consultants ( AKA BDR) are hired to create opportunities. 

**1.3 Ancillary / Referral Relationships**

Beyond its two core products, Howie Golden has personal broker relationships with outside financial institutions that can provide law firms with lines of credit and other financial solutions Capital Financing does not itself offer, including post-settlement financing. These relationships are a marketing opportunity in their own right and are not currently reflected anywhere on the website or in outbound marketing.  Howie is considering creating a new entity to market separately for this service but can also be marketed on CF website strategically.

**4. Priority Areas for Improvement**

These are the areas Capital Financing most wants The Marketing Boss's help thinking through. The common thread across nearly all of them: shift the center of gravity of marketing spend and effort toward law firms, and away from anything aimed at plaintiffs.

**4.1 Reorient Marketing Toward Law Firms, Not Plaintiffs**

The single biggest strategic ask: Capital Financing does not want to spend marketing dollars competing for plaintiff attention. All new marketing investment should be evaluated against whether it builds awareness, trust, and referral relationships with law firms.

**4.2 Website / Landing Page Strategy**

- The current website is optimized for plaintiffs, not focused for law firms researching Capital Financing's case expense financing or pre-settlement services.  I hope KJ has built the SEO for these services, but law firms don’t see pre-settlement services but will seek case expense services which we need to improve.  
- Howie is considering a separate, purpose-built landing page (or even a separate brand/site) specifically for specialty litigation financing unrelated to CF, targeted at law firms — a more creative, niche B2B approach distinct from the existing plaintiff-facing site.  While also branding the CF ancillary services. 
- Any new site or page should also make room for the ancillary line-of-credit / post-settlement financing referral relationships described in Section 1.3, which aren't marketed anywhere today.

**4.3 Content Strategy**

Howie considers the current social posting cadence largely a waste of time relative to its branding benefit and is not satisfied with its effectiveness. He is open to a different content approach — including video — that is genuinely built to earn attention from a law firm audience, rather than a generic posting cadence.  Focus should be on the expertise of Howie, the reputation, innovation, disruption, and impact CF has had on the industry.  Howie is often sought on podcasts and webinar’s for the expertise and approach to financing for the future of law firms.  Also, CF has been in business for 16 years which shows brand loyalty, reputation, and trust in the community.  Also, being Margarita has an expertise marketing to law firms, I am hoping she has a different approach to create opportunities.  

**4.4 Conference ROI & Pre/During/Post System**

With 15–20 conferences a year at real expense, building a proper pre-conference, during-conference, and post-conference marketing system — and a reliable way to trace eventual referrals back to a specific conference contact — is a top priority. This will need close coordination with Kaz, who owns both the Mailchimp rebuild and the attendee-level Salesforce reporting.  How can we improve opportunities for an audience that should be easy being we were attending the same conference.  How can we get the emails needed to market and how do we approach calls vs. emails. I would love video broadcasts to these attendees pre, during, and post conferences that can be powerful.  Need best way to create and deliver.  

**4.5 Email Automation & AI-Assisted Outreach**

Howie sees an opportunity to replace much of Julius's manual email work — prospecting, reactivation, thank you emails, active clients, post conference, and drip touches — with AI-assisted or better-automated email generation and sequencing, freeing capacity and improving consistency of law firm touchpoints.

**4.6 Julius's Role: Fit and Future**

Howie wants a clear-eyed look at whether Julius's current marketing output (email and social) is actually moving the business, given that he does not make outbound calls and the social media effort is not seen as effective. Two related questions are on the table:

- Whether Julius should be repositioned as an executive assistant — a pivot previously discussed with Capital Financing's former COO.
- Whether a different resource — potentially a new virtual assistant trained specifically for it, functioning more like an inside-sales / outreach role — is needed to drive the consistent, numbers-based outreach that the Financial Consultants are not currently doing enough of themselves.

**4.7 Financial Consultant KPIs & Sales Management Gap**

There is no dedicated sales manager for the Financial Consultant team; Howie is currently absorbing that responsibility himself. Kaz is building KPI reporting for the team, and this is called out as a priority area where The Marketing Boss's team can help — both in defining the right KPIs and in supporting the reporting and accountability structure around them.

**4.8 Intake Training & SOP Development**

Christy trains intake staff well today, but there is no formal SOP or documented workflow for the department. She along with Yasmine who is a director for 2 departments overseen by Christy, is expected to complete SOP’s for JC.  We also need SOPs as it will drive our new software we are seeking to replace current financing software (Mighty to Segue).  As the team grows, a written SOP would help new hires ramp faster and more consistently, while also driving the build of new software that will integrate with Salesforce. This is a smaller, complementary opportunity for collaboration alongside the marketing-focused priorities above.

**4.9 Marketing the Ancillary Relationships**

Howie's personal relationships with outside financial institutions — which can get law firms lines of credit and other solutions like post-settlement financing that Capital Financing does not itself provide — represent a marketing opportunity that isn't being pursued today and isn't reflected on the website and where Howie wants to spend money to get law firms seeking these services to reach out.
