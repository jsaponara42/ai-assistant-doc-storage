### Notes on ranking
- Rank reflects **build/implementation effort**, not business impact — the portal (#6) and the follow-up automation (#9) are likely Howie's highest-impact asks given his stated concerns about submission friction (#6) and consultants not following up on accounts (#9).
- #7 and #8 are related (territory assignment logic in #7 depends on the territory definitions being finalized in #8) — #8 should probably be resolved directionally before #7 is fully built out.
- #9 (follow-up automation) is a cross-cutting concern that touches all consultants' pipelines, not just Howie's. It may need to be scoped separately from the CEO's own daily routine (see `SOPs/20260728_Capital-Financing_Daily-Salesforce-Task-Review.md`) — the SOP documents what Howie does manually; #9 is about automating that behavior for the entire sales staff.
- #1 and #2 (the two 2026-08-05 additions) are both effectively zero-build — ranked ahead of everything else on pure ease, not because they outrank the others in importance.
- #3 (AI notetaker) is also low-build, but unlike #1/#2 it's an adoption problem, not a one-time task — getting the whole team to actually use it consistently is the real work, not picking the tool.
