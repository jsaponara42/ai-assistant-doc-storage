See the workflow map's Sales Performance & Accountability Framework for full detail.
- **Historical caution for any new email-automation work (Kaz call, 2026-08-XX):** Capital Financing previously used a native Salesforce email-marketing tool that was badly buggy (couldn't reliably paste an image into an email without it going blank) — Howie stopped paying for it and it was shut off. Worth remembering before recommending replacement tooling for the current ~18 drip campaigns or any new automated sequence: the team has already been burned once by an under-vetted email tool, so a confident recommendation matters more here than usual.
- **Contact scale, for context on #8/#9 (Kaz call, 2026-08-XX):** Salesforce currently holds roughly 25,000 contacts and 10,000 companies. - #1 and #2 (the two 2026-08-05 additions) are both effectively zero-build — ranked ahead of everything else on pure ease, not because they outrank the others in importance.
- #3 (AI notetaker) is also low-build, but unlike #1/#2 it's an adoption problem, not a one-time task — getting the whole team to actually use it consistently is the real work, not picking the tool.
