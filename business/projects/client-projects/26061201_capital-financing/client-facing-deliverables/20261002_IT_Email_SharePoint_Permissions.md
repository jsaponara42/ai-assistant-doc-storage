## Content

**Subject:** SharePoint: I can't delete folders and can't limit who sees what

Hi [IT contact name],

I'm an owner of our Capital Financing SharePoint site and I'm stuck on two things I can't fix myself. We're about to build more on this site, so I'd like both sorted first.

**1. I can't delete things I own**

Library settings list me as an owner with full control. Even so, I can't delete a number of the older folders on the site, including empty ones like General and PDF that were created years ago. Folders I create today delete fine. I was able to clear some of the old ones, but the rest won't go. One item also shows as checked out, with a small red arrow. I wonder if a retention policy, a hold, or a checked-out file is blocking me, but I can't see where to look. Christy now has owner access too and may run into the same thing.

**2. Everyone gets access to everything**

When I create a new folder, everyone in the company gets access automatically. I tested this with a new folder and it happened right away. Our site was set up this way by a former employee. Some of what lives here should not be visible to the whole staff, and I can't find a way to give a specific folder to specific people. I also can't see who currently has access to what. The member list shows groups and accounts I don't recognize, and I'd like to know what they are, including any that belong to an outside vendor.

**What I'd like the site to look like when this is done**

1. I can delete any file or folder on the site, and so can the person I name as backup owner (Christy). If a retention policy or hold is the reason something can't be deleted, you tell me what it is and clear it where it's safe to.
2. Access is set by folder. I can give a folder to one person, a small group, or everyone, and new folders do not default to the whole company.
3. Staff are in a few clear groups (for example leadership, administration, financial consultants) so I can give access to a group and not to individuals.
4. I can see, at any time, who has access to which folders, and I know how to change it myself.
5. The old empty folders are gone.
6. You give me a short written summary of how access is set up now, so it doesn't drift back to what we have today.

**One more thing**

We use Microsoft 365 with an AI tool (Claude) connected to our SharePoint and Outlook. It works with everything except Microsoft Planner, which it can't reach. Can you tell me whether that's an admin approval, a license, or a permission, and what it would take to turn on?

**How I'd like you to reply**

Please sort your answer into two lists:

- **What IT can fix or explain.** Permissions, retention policies, admin settings, group setup, the Planner access.
- **What is not an IT issue.** Anything that comes down to a decision or a task on our side, like which folders to keep, who should see what, how files are named, or how the team uses the site.

That way I know exactly what I have to solve myself.

If it's easier to walk through this on a screen share, I'm happy to do that. Thanks.

Howie
