## Summary

Build a lead magnet tool: prospect pastes a SharePoint/Google Drive folder or file link, and the tool shows a side-by-side — "what AI sees" (messy, current state) vs. "what AI could see" (organized, post-taxonomy state). Makes the file-chaos pain point tangible instead of abstract, and doubles as a live demo of the offering itself.

## Context

Surfaced 2026-08-14 alongside [[business/marketing/offers/20260814_file-chaos-marketing-angles]] and the file-chaos cost calculator ([[tasks/jc-tasks/4-2026-08-14-build-file-chaos-cost-calculator]]). Same funnel role as the calculator (Decoy-style: free/limited tool feeding the paid taxonomy engagement) but more visceral — shows rather than tells.

## Content

Open design question, not yet resolved: would a prospect actually paste a real corporate SharePoint/Drive link into an unknown tool? Likely not for a live production system — trust/security concern. Two directions to consider:
- **Illustrative/staged version** — a fixed, representative "messy folder" demo (not the prospect's real data) that shows the before/after concept generically. Lower trust barrier, faster to build, less personalized.
- **Real but scoped-down version** — accept a single public share-link or a small test folder only, not a full drive — real but bounded, lower risk than asking for full access.

Either direction should pair with the file-chaos cost calculator and pain points from the marketing angles note as supporting copy.
