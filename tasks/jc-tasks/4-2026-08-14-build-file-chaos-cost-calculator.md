## Summary

Build the file-chaos cost calculator flagged in [[business/marketing/offers/20260814_file-chaos-marketing-angles]] — an interactive tool that estimates how much time/money a company loses to disorganized files, personalized by team size, as a lead magnet feeding the Information Taxonomy offering.

## Context

Surfaced during the 2026-08-14 taxonomy-offering strategy session. Grounded in real research (McKinsey: employees spend roughly 1.8 hrs/day searching for information) rather than invented numbers — see the marketing angles note for the full citation and illustrative math. Also connects to the earlier diagnostic-quiz/calculator concept from the original lead-magnet brainstorm ([[business/marketing/offers/20260814_attraction-offer-concepts]]).

## Content

Rough shape:
- Inputs: team size, rough loaded cost per employee (or revenue-based estimate if that's easier for a prospect to answer)
- Calculation: ~9.3 hrs/week per employee × 52 weeks × loaded hourly cost = estimated annual cost of search time
- Output: a personalized dollar figure + a CTA to book a Roadmap/taxonomy call
- Consider pairing with 2-3 of the pain points from the marketing angles note as supporting copy around the result
